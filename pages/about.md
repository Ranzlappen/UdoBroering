## Hey there

This is a personal blog — a space for passion projects, random experiments,
deep dives into things I find interesting, and the occasional hot take on
something nobody asked about. No single niche, no editorial calendar, just
genuine curiosity put into words... 

## What you'll find here

Topics range from tech and tinkering to whatever rabbit hole I fell into last
weekend. Posts are organized by **category** and **tags**, and a few are grouped
into multi-part **series**. Use the search (press <kbd>Ctrl</kbd>/<kbd>⌘</kbd>+<kbd>K</kbd>)
to dig through the archive.

## Built with Jekyll

This site is a static blog built with [Jekyll](https://jekyllrb.com/) and hosted
on GitHub Pages — no database, no tracking, no build step beyond Jekyll itself.
It ships with dark/light themes, an installable PWA + offline support, RSS, full
SEO metadata, and client-side search.

> **Note:** This is a starter template. Update the site name, author, and social
> links in `_config.yml`, replace the placeholder icons in `/icons/`, and start
> writing in `_posts/`.
