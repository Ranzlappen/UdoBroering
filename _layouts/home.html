Essays and papers on philosophy — the questions that have occupied the great thinkers from antiquity to the present, and why they still shape how we understand the world.

Essays and papers

This site gathers two kinds of writing: long-form essays that follow a single idea wherever it leads, and longer academic papers you can read online or download as a PDF. Both trace how thinkers ancient and modern have asked what we can know, how we ought to live, and what it means to understand the world around us.

Why the old questions still matter

Philosophy is not a museum. The arguments that shaped earlier ages keep returning because the questions behind them are still ours — about knowledge, nature, ethics, and meaning. These pages revisit the great thinkers not as relics but as conversation partners, read closely enough to see why their ideas endure. Start with the essays or browse the papers.
